Challenges

For grown-ups

2,533 words in five levels: Level 1 (grades 1–2), Level 2 (grades 3–4), Level 3 (new grade 5–6 words plus grade 5), Level 4 (the next 400 grade 5–6 words) and Level 5 (the hardest grade 6 words). Grade 1–5 words come from the Basic Spelling Vocabulary List (Graham, Harris & Loynachan, 1993) plus extra grade 4 and 5 words. The grade 5–6 words were gathered from HMH Journeys, McGraw-Hill Reading Wonders, the UK National Curriculum Years 5–6 list, the Scripps school spelling bee lists, K5 Learning and Vocabulary.com, keeping only common words. Inside each level, words go from easy and common to harder and rarer. A 21-word level test (spelling and sentence questions) decides where to start. Every session is 10 words: mistake words from earlier days come first, then new words in order. New pets hatch from a mystery egg roughly every 30 sessions, and each pet has a hidden limited-edition skin. A word pays $1 only when it is right in all three rounds; missed words come back from the next day on, worth $2. Playing one session a day keeps the streak. Progress saves automatically on this device and to your Claude account when signed in.

Quit this session? Coins from it won't be saved.
